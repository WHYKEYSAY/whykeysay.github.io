What it does

Deployment Package

Assembles the artifacts, configs and checks that define a release into one coherent, reviewable package.

Evidence-Closure Report

Turns "is this ready?" into a verifiable record — every readiness claim backed by evidence, gaps flagged before they reach production.

Human sign-off

Auriga verifies completeness; a person signs. The gate keeps accountability with the engineer while removing the guesswork.

The gate

Deployment package→ Readiness checks→ Evidence closure→ Human signs→ Ship
