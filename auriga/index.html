What it does

Three things, before anything ships.

01

Deployment package

Assembles the artifacts, configs, and checks that define a release into one coherent, reviewable record — the thing the gate actually inspects.

02

Evidence-closure report

Turns “is this ready?” into a verifiable record. Every readiness claim is backed by evidence; gaps are flagged before they reach production.

03

Human sign-off

Auriga verifies completeness; a person signs. The gate keeps accountability with the engineer and removes the guesswork, not the judgment.

Honest assessment

What the gate demonstrates — and what it does not.

It does

  • Make release readiness an explicit, auditable record instead of a verbal “looks good”.
  • Bind every readiness claim to evidence, and surface the gaps before production.
  • Keep the final signature — and the accountability — with a human.

It does not

  • Decide whether the release should happen. The gate checks completeness, not intent.
  • Replace tests, review, or judgment — it records that they closed.
  • Claim production scale yet. It runs as a single self-hosted service on the cluster.
